# Stage: topic-research — gap-driven, 2-source, stop condition

> Runs inside `generate-pptx.md` phase 4. Output is `research-notes.md`,
> one section per slide: 4–8 content bullets + 2-source log. Ship-blocking.

## Procedure

1. List every headline stat, date, name, and proper noun the outline
   needs. Each becomes a gap line: `GAP-1: Ha Long Bay island count`.
2. Fill each gap from two independent sources. Record both in the
   source log. One source = `[unverified]`, kept off headline slides.
3. Version-check every API, command, and version number against the
   installed tool or current docs. Installed truth wins over docs.
4. Stop when every slide section holds ~60 words of distinct facts.
   More research past that is procrastination.

## Handoff

`Research done: N/N gaps sourced, M [unverified] — outline may proceed.`
