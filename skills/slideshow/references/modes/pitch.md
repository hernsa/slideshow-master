# Mode: pitch — 8 slides, investor arc

Eight slides, one ask. Every slide answers the question the investor is
already thinking. No appendix in the main flow — appendix rides `none`
transition at the back.

## Arc (8 slides)

1. Title — photo-hero: company + one-line outcome. Notes: the 15-second
   version.
2. Problem — display-type: cost of the status quo, one number.
3. Solution — showcase: product raster on near-black, one accent.
4. How it wins — duo boxes: two differentiators, named, morph anchors.
5. Traction — giant stat + tiles. Real numbers or omit the slide.
6. Business model — 3 flow pills: who pays, for what, how often.
7. Team — 2–3 avatar circle_pic tiles + one line each. No resumes.
8. Ask — framing close: amount, use, contact. Notes: the exact ask,
   word for word.

## PPTX notes

- Moving object: the product raster (slides 1 → 3 → 5).
- Stats morph-tween across 2 → 5 where the digits persist.
- One transition for the deck; the ask slide is never the place for a
  transition exception.
